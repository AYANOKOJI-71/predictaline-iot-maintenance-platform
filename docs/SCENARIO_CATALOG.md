# Scenario Catalogue

| Scenario | Synthetic signal pattern | User-facing evidence | Conservative validation step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steady state | Sensor values remain inside the demo baseline envelope. | Low anomaly signal and no domain guard. | Continue ordinary planned inspection. |
| Thermal drift | Temperature rises across successive fixture events. | Temperature trend and elevated anomaly score. | Confirm sensor calibration and inspect cooling conditions. |
| Bearing wear | Vibration and current draw rise together in a repeatable pattern. | Coupled vibration/current factors and high anomaly signal. | Use a qualified technician to inspect lubrication, alignment, and bearing condition. |
| Pressure instability | Pressure varies beyond the fixture envelope while vibration changes. | Pressure variability factor and supporting vibration evidence. | Validate instrument readings and inspect the relevant fluid path under site procedures. |

These scenarios are intentionally fictional. The recommendations are conservative review prompts, not instructions to operate, repair, or bypass equipment.
